Legacy Link Android

Your phone's music on the car screen

Legacy Link puts what's playing on this car's screen: album art, up next and controls. Music plays through the car's Bluetooth, or bit-exact over Wi-Fi. Skip and pause from the steering wheel. Android phones only.

Download for Android

Free · version 0.9.1 · about 8 MB · Android 9 or newer · installs directly, not from the Play Store

Optional bit-exact Wi-Fi audio, no Bluetooth squeeze
Covers, up next and controls on the car

Rather not? Bluetooth works as it always has: pair your phone with the car the normal way.

Three steps, once

After this it connects by itself every time you get in.

  1. 1

    Install the app

    Tap the download, then open the file. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser: allow it once.

  2. 2

    Turn on your hotspot

    The car joins your phone's hotspot; that is how the music reaches the screen. The link itself uses no mobile data (your music app uses data as it always does).

  3. 3

    Open Legacy Link, tap Get started

    It walks you through the rest. The first time, the car screen shows a code to confirm on your phone; allow the prompts the app explains, and play something.

What it asks for, and why

Hotspot
The car has no internet of its own. It joins your phone to receive the music.
Notification access
So the car screen can show the title, artist and cover of what's playing.
"Start recording or casting"
Only for Wi-Fi audio. Android's only way to let an app pass the sound through. Legacy Link only carries the audio to the car; nothing is recorded or saved.
A personal project for one car. Updates arrive inside the app (Settings → About). Releases: github.com/dovydasv2/Legacy_Link-releases
