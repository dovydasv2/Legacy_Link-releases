Legacy Link Privacy

Privacy policy

Legacy Link links your Android phone with your car's Android head unit over your own Wi-Fi. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. What it uses stays on your phone and your head unit.

Last updated 8 November 2026 · applies to the Google Play and GitHub versions

What the app uses, and why

What your media apps are playing
Through Android's notification access: title, artist, album, album art, position and playback controls. Sent only to your head unit, so it can show and control what plays. Your other notifications are not read, stored or shared.
The sound your media apps play (optional Wi-Fi audio)
Captured through Android's screen-share permission and streamed, encrypted, straight to your head unit. Never the microphone. Nothing is recorded or stored.
Approximate location (optional)
Only to work out today's sunrise and sunset for the head unit's night mode. Only those two times go to the head unit; your location is not stored or shared.
Bluetooth device names
So you can pick which Bluetooth device is your car; Legacy Link then saves power when you're not in the car. Stored on the phone.
Installed apps
Names and icons of your media apps (to show where music is playing from), and on the head unit, its apps (to open its Bluetooth music app). Not stored beyond your settings, not shared.
Wi-Fi and network state
To find and keep the link between your phone's hotspot and the head unit.

What leaves your devices

To your head unit only
Everything above travels over your phone's hotspot to your own head unit, encrypted once the two are paired. Nothing goes to the developer or any third party.
Album covers from your music app
When a music app gives only a web address for a cover, Legacy Link downloads that picture from that address, as the music app itself would.
Update checks (GitHub version only)
The GitHub version asks github.com for new versions and downloads them; GitHub sees an ordinary anonymous request. The Google Play version is updated by Google Play and makes no such requests.
Google Play purchases (if offered)
Handled entirely by Google Play; Legacy Link only learns whether a purchase was made.

What's kept, and for how long

Settings, pairings, the last played track and its cover, recent album names and a local diagnostic log stay on your phone and head unit until you change them or uninstall the app. Android's own device backup may include the settings if you have backup turned on. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Children

Legacy Link is a car accessory app and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact
